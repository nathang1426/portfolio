Lab 6

“Take It Easy.” Integrated Campaign

One big idea across four touchpoints: our flip-flops aren't just for the beach. They're an easy, comfortable part of everyday life.

Campaign ideaTVOut-of-homeSocialExperiential

1What it is

The campaign is built around the tagline “Take It Easy.” Whether you're hanging out with friends, heading to class, or spending time outside, Coastal Threads encourages people to slow down and enjoy the moment. It focuses on comfort, simplicity, and freedom, which reflect the brand's laid-back personality.

TV commercial

“A Day Made Easier”

A college student rushes through a stressful day of class and responsibilities, then changes into our flip-flops and heads outside to meet friends. The music relaxes as everyone hangs out. Closing line: “Life moves fast. Take It Easy.”

Out-of-home

“Less Stress. More Steps.”

Posters and billboards near college campuses and popular outdoor spots, showing a pair of our flip-flops on a dock over the water, with the logo and tagline underneath.

Social media

#TakeItEasy

A TikTok and Instagram campaign inviting people to share their favorite ways to relax in our flip-flops, with the brand reposting favorites to build engagement.

Experiential

“Take It Easy” Pop-Up

A branded relaxation spot on campuses and at outdoor events with lawn chairs, games, music, try-ons, and a photo backdrop, so people can experience the lifestyle instead of only seeing it.

2Process

  1. Went back to the brand personality from Lab 1 (relaxed and authentic) to find one idea the whole campaign could share.
  2. Landed on “Take It Easy.” and the insight that flip-flops can be an everyday choice, not just a beach item.
  3. Matched each channel to our 18–35 audience: a relatable college story for TV, billboards near campuses, user-made content on social, and a hands-on pop-up.
  4. Wrote the concept for each touchpoint so they all end on the same tagline.

The challenge

Making four very different channels feel like one campaign. A TV spot, a billboard, a hashtag, and an event all work differently, and it was easy for them to drift into separate ideas.

How I solved it

I used “Take It Easy.” as the anchor for everything: every piece either ends on the tagline or is built around the feeling of slowing down, so each one reinforces the others.

3Reflection

What I learned

A strong campaign starts with one simple idea. Once that idea was clear, deciding what each channel should do got much easier. I also learned how important it is to meet the audience where they already are.

What I'd improve

I'd build out real creative for each piece, like a storyboard for the commercial and sample social posts, and set clear goals (reach, engagement, pop-up visits) to measure the campaign's success.
